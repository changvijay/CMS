1. Signing in

# Label What to do
1 Username or email Type your username (e.g. chang) or your email address.

3. Overview — all platforms at once

Controls and totals

# Label What it does
1 Date range Choose 7, 14 or 30 days. Every number is compared with the same number of days just before.
2 Download CSV Downloads the numbers as a spreadsheet (opens in Excel / Google Sheets).
3 Print / PDF Print the page, or choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
4 Refresh now (Update all data) Fetches the latest numbers from every platform immediately.
5 Quick insights Plain sentences such as "Instagram drives 93% of views".
6 Combined totals Views, interactions and new followers across all platforms. ▲ green = up, ▼ red = down vs the previous period.

Each account at a glance

# Label What it means
1 One card per account Every connected Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and Facebook account gets a card.
2 Platform + account name Which platform and which account the card is for.
3 Open workspace Click Open ↗ to see that account in detail (Figure 7).
4 Numbers vs previous period Key numbers with % change. If the card says "No data stored…", open it and press Update data.

Charts and best content

# Label What it shows
1 Daily views per platform Line chart of daily views, one colour per platform.
2 Share of views Which platform brings most of your views.
3 Side-by-side table Views, interactions and new followers per platform, with % change.
4 Top posts Your best posts/videos by engagement rate (only items with 100+ views).

LinkedIn impressions count as views here. Facebook numbers are read live.

4. Platform workspace basics

Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn pages all share the same layout.

# Label What to do
1 Profile Account name, handle and bio.
2 Lifetime numbers Followers, following and posts right now.
3 Choose account If you have several accounts on this platform, pick one here.
4 Date range 7 / 14 / 30 days, or Custom for exact dates (custom ranges load live and can be slower).
5 Last refreshed When the numbers were last updated.
6 Update data Click to refresh now (takes a few seconds).
7 Tabs Overview, Content, Audience, Strategy, Research, Data (each explained below).

5. Instagram

Content tab

# Label What to do
1 Search captions Find a post by words in its caption.
2 Sort Newest, most views, best engagement, most likes/comments/saves/shares, lowest skip rate, longest watch time.
3 Grid / List Switch between cards and a compact list.
4 Filter by type All posts, Reels, Stories, Carousels.
5 Post card Views, reach, likes, comments, saves, shares, skip rate and average watch time for one post.
6 Open on Instagram Opens the post in Instagram.
7 Add auto-reply Opens the auto-reply form already set to this post (Figure 24).
8 Comment insights Summary of the comments on this post.

Stories: Instagram keeps story numbers for only 24 hours. The app saves them every 6 hours (and whenever you press Update data) so you keep their history.

Audience tab

# Label What it tells you
1 Followers vs new people How many accounts reached were followers vs people who don't follow you. High share of people who don't follow you = you're being discovered.
2 Views split Same split for views.
3 Views by format Views from reels, posts and stories.
4 Reach by format Which format finds new people.

Further down this tab: follows vs unfollows, saves/shares/likes/comments by format, Best time to post heat map, and follower age, gender, countries and cities.

Instagram only provides demographics and "best time to post" for accounts with 100+ followers.

Strategy tab

# Label What it means

2 Recommendation One tip, with the numbers behind it (e.g. "Post more consistently").
3 Priority High priority, Medium or Nice to have — start with High.
# Label What it means

2 Copy these openings Your reels with the lowest skip rate — reuse their style.
3 Fix these openings Your reels with the highest skip rate — rework the first 3 seconds.

Below this: the Format scorecard comparing reels, carousels and images.

9. Social Post — publish everywhere

Write once, publish (or schedule) to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube. Steps: Platform → Account → Content → Auto reply → Review. Your work is saved as a draft automatically.

Step 1 — Choose platforms

# Label What to do
1 Your posts and drafts Click to reopen a draft, see scheduled/finished posts, or delete a draft.
2 Steps Shows where you are in the 5 steps.
3 Click to select Click a platform card (tick appears).
4 Pick more to cross-post Select several platforms to post the same content everywhere.
5 Save draft Save and come back later.
6 Next step Go to step 2.

Post types per platform

Platform Post types
Instagram Image, Carousel (2–10 items), Reel, Story
Facebook Page Text, Link, Photo, Multi-photo, Video, Reel (3–90 s), Story
LinkedIn Text only, Image, Multi-image (2–20), Video, Document (PDF), Article, Poll (2–4 options)
YouTube Video (vertical, 3 minutes or less = Short)

Step 2 — Choose accounts

# Label What to do
1 Tick the account(s) Pick which account(s) to post from.
2 Back Return to step 1.
3 Next Go to step 3.

Step 3 — Write the post

# Label What to do
1 Shared fields (fill once) Title, link, description, hashtags, tags, media, thumbnail, location. They copy to every platform tab, trimmed to each platform's limits.
2 Schedule (empty = now) Leave empty to publish immediately, or pick a future date/time. Facebook schedules must be 10 minutes to 30 days ahead.
3 Platform tab One tab per platform for its own options. A red number = something to fix (the message appears in red at the bottom of the tab).
4 Post type e.g. Image, Carousel, Reel, Story.
5 Live preview Roughly how the post will look.
6 Caption Text for this platform. Synced = it follows the shared field. Counters show limits (e.g. 0 / 2,200, # 0 / 30).

Then click Next.

Step 4 — Auto reply (Instagram only, optional)

Turn it on to answer comments on this new post automatically:

  1. Public reply under the comment — up to 10 variants; one is chosen at random. {username} becomes @their_name.
  2. Private reply by DM — message, link, up to 3 link buttons, quick replies. {{link}} places the link.
  3. Ask them to follow before sending the link (optional) — they get an "I followed ✅" button; the link is sent after the app checks they follow.

Step 5 — Review and publish

Check the summary, then click Publish now (or Schedule for …). You'll see live progress per platform (Upload → Create → Processing → Publish). If one platform fails, click Retry for just that platform.

10. Auto-replies (Instagram)

Open from Account menu → Auto-replies (Figure 3 → 3). When someone comments or DMs a keyword (e.g. "link"), the app sends them your message and URL automatically.

Rules list

# Label What to do
1 Setup check / Try it / Activity Opens the tools in Figures 26–27.
2 Create a rule Click New rule (Figure 24).
3 Filter rules All rules, or only rules for one reel/post.
4 On / off Untick to pause a rule without deleting it.
5 Which reel / post The content the rule applies to. Open ↗ shows it on Instagram.
6 Keyword The trigger word and how it's matched.
7 Reply sent The message people receive. The Sent column counts replies sent.
8 Edit / Delete Change or remove the rule.

Create a rule

# Label What to do
1 Account Choose the Instagram account.
2 Applies to Everything (comments + DMs), All my reels, All my posts, or One specific reel or post (pick from thumbnails).
3 Trigger words Keywords separated by commas, e.g. link, pdf, லிங்க்.
4 Match type Contains the word, Starts with, or Is exactly.
5 Reply to Comments (sends a DM) and/or Direct messages.
6 Your message Use {{link}} for the link and {{username}} for their name.
# Label What to do
1 Your link The URL to send (must start with https://).
2 Optional public reply A short public reply under the comment, posted only after the DM succeeds.
3 Cool-down (hours) Don't message the same person again within this many hours.
4 Disclose automation Adds "(Automated reply)" — recommended by Instagram.
5 Preview Exactly what they will receive.
6 Save the rule Click Create rule.

Which rule wins? The most specific: one reel/post rule → all reels/posts rule → Everything rule. Reel/post rules only answer comments (a DM can't be linked to a post).

Shortcut: Instagram → Content → Set up auto-reply for this reel (Figure 8 → 7) opens this form already pointed at that post.

Setup check

# Label What to do
1 Switch tool Move between Setup check, Try it and Activity.
2 Server checks (must be ✓) Everything must be green ✓ for replies to be sent.
3 Fix an account If an account shows ✕, click Subscribe again.

Try it (safe test)

# Label What to do
1 Account Pick the account to test.
2 Comment or DM Simulate a comment or a direct message.
3 Which content Any reel, any post, or a specific one.
4 What they write Type a sample message, e.g. link please.
5 Test (sends nothing) Shows which rule would answer and what would be sent. Nothing is sent to anyone.

Activity (third tool) lists the latest messages handled, with Instagram's error code if something failed. Click Refresh to reload.

Instagram's limits: one private reply (DM) per comment, within 7 days of the comment; DMs can be answered within 24 hours.

13. How the data is updated

When What happens
Every night, 2:30 AM IST Latest daily numbers are saved for every connected account.
Every 6 hours Instagram stories are captured (Instagram deletes story numbers after 24 h).
Every minute Scheduled posts are checked and sent at their time.
When you click Update data / Update all data Numbers refresh immediately.

Preset ranges (7/14/30 days) use saved history; Custom ranges are fetched live. All times are in India Standard Time (IST).

14. Glossary

Term Meaning
Reach Number of different accounts that saw your content.
Views / Impressions Times content was shown or played (one person can count more than once).
Interactions / Engagements Likes + comments + saves + shares (plus reactions/clicks where counted).
Engagement rate Interactions as a share of views (or reach).
Net followers New follows minus unfollows.
Skip rate % of reel plays stopped in the first 3 seconds. Lower = better hook.
Avg % viewed How much of each video people watched on average.
Watch time Total minutes/hours watched.
Link taps Taps on profile link/contact buttons.
Red dot (unusual day) A day far outside your normal range (more than 2 standard deviations).
vs prev period Compared with the same number of days just before.

15. Troubleshooting and FAQ

"No data stored yet for this period" — press Update data (Figure 7 → 6). After that it refreshes every night.

Audience/demographics are empty — Instagram only shares these for accounts with 100+ followers.

An account stopped updating — Connected accounts → Update token or Reconnect (Figure 28 → 3).

Auto-replies aren't being sent

  1. Setup check must be all green ✓ — click Subscribe again if not (Figure 26 → 3).
  2. Rule must be On (Figure 23 → 4). Test it in Try it (Figure 27).
  3. Look at Activity for Instagram's error code.
  4. Limits: one DM per comment within 7 days; DMs answered within 24 hours.

A post failed on one platform — on the progress screen click Retry for that platform, or reopen it from Your posts and drafts (Figure 20 → 1).

Red number on a platform tab / "Image needs an image" — each post type needs the right media (e.g. Reel = one video, Carousel = 2–10 items). Fix the red message and the number disappears.

Times look wrong — all times are in India Standard Time (IST).

Forgot password — contact your administrator.
