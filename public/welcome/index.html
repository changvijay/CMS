FAQ

Questions, answered

Which accounts can I connect?

Instagram Professional (Business or Creator) accounts, Facebook Pages, YouTube channels and LinkedIn company pages. You can connect several of each.

How fresh is the data?

Every account is refreshed every night at 2:30 AM IST. You can also press Update data anytime to load the latest numbers in a few seconds.

How do Instagram auto-replies work?

You create a rule with keywords, a message and a link. When someone comments or sends a DM with that keyword, the studio replies automatically. Instagram allows one private reply per comment within 7 days, and DM replies within 24 hours.

Can I schedule posts?

Yes. Set a schedule time in the Social Post wizard, or leave it empty to publish immediately. Facebook scheduled posts must be 10 minutes to 30 days ahead.

Why are my audience demographics empty?

Instagram only shares demographics and "best time to post" for accounts with at least 100 followers.

The user manual walks through every screen with labelled screenshots.
