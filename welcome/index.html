FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does the studio cost?

₹7,999 a year plus GST for one brand on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn. Every feature is included and there are no per account fees.

Which accounts can I connect?

Instagram Professional accounts (Business or Creator), Facebook Pages, YouTube channels and LinkedIn company pages. You can connect several of each.

How fresh are the numbers?

Every account is refreshed every night at 2:30 AM IST. Press Update data at any time to load the latest numbers in a few seconds.

How do Instagram auto replies work?

You create a rule with keywords, a message and a link. When someone comments or sends a DM with that keyword, the studio replies automatically. Instagram allows one private reply per comment within 7 days, and DM replies within 24 hours.

Can I schedule posts?

Yes. Pick a schedule time in the Social Post wizard, or leave it empty to publish right away. Facebook scheduled posts must be 10 minutes to 30 days ahead.

Why are my audience demographics empty?

Instagram only shares demographics and best time to post for accounts with at least 100 followers.

What happens when a connection expires?

Connections renew themselves. If a platform refuses the renewal, the Accounts page shows it and you press Update token or Reconnect.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Every platform has a Data tab with a CSV download, and the Overview page can be printed or saved as a PDF.

Where can I learn every feature?

The user manual walks through every screen with labelled screenshots, and is also available as a Word file.
