Privacy Policy

For the CMS Studio app (Android and iOS) and CMS Analytics Studio at cms.changtechs.com. Effective 17 November 2026.

The short version

  • We collect what is needed to run your studio: your sign-in details, the social accounts you connect, their statistics, and the posts and media you create.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no crash reporting. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • The app only reads photos and videos you pick yourself. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.
  • You can disconnect any account in the app, and ask us to delete everything by email.

CMS Studio is the mobile app for CMS Analytics Studio, a product of Chang IT Solution ("we", "us"). It lets you see analytics for your Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn accounts, publish and schedule posts to them, and set up automatic replies to Instagram comments. The app and the website share one account and one server, so this policy covers both.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Sign-in details. Your username or email address and your password. Accounts are created for you by us; the password is stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt), never in readable form.
  • Waiting list. If you join the waiting list, we collect your email address, your phone number, how many accounts you want to connect on each platform, and any profile links you enter.
  • Posts and media. Captions, titles, descriptions, hashtags, schedules, drafts, location tags and other post settings, and the photos and videos you choose to attach.
  • Auto-reply rules. The keywords, replies and messages you set up.

Information from the accounts you connect

When you connect an Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or LinkedIn account, you sign in on that platform's own page and approve access. We then receive and store:

  • Access tokens that let our server act for you on that platform within the permissions you approved.
  • Profile details such as the account name, username, ID and profile picture.
  • Statistics such as views, reach, followers, engagement, audience breakdowns (for example age, gender, country or city in aggregate, as the platform reports them), video retention and, for YouTube channels that have it, estimated revenue.
  • Your content and its comments, such as your posts, reels, stories, videos and playlists, and comments on them, used for the content and comment summaries in the app.
  • For auto-replies: when someone comments on or messages your Instagram account, we receive their comment or message text, username and account ID so a matching rule can reply. These events are kept in the auto-reply activity log you can see in the app.

Information on your phone

  • Photos and videos are read only when you pick them with your phone's photo picker to attach to a post. The app does not browse or upload anything else.
  • Your session key is kept in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain). Small preferences, such as your last publishing choices, are kept in the app's own storage on the phone.
  • Home screen widgets show your recent numbers and upcoming posts. That data stays on your phone.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, call logs, advertising ID or browsing history, and it does not use third-party analytics, advertising or crash reporting tools. Like any internet service, our server receives your IP address and basic request details when the app talks to it.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To fetch and store your statistics, and show them as charts, insights, best posting times and a content strategy.
  • To publish or schedule posts to the accounts you choose, only when you submit them.
  • To send the automatic comment replies and direct messages you set up.
  • To summarise comments (for example the most-used words and how many are questions). This is done on our own server; your comments are not sent to any outside AI service.
  • To contact you about your waiting list request, your account or the service.
  • To find and fix problems, and to keep the service secure.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles of you for anyone else.

4. How long we keep it and how we protect it

  • Daily statistics are kept for 30 days and older days are deleted automatically every night. For older posts we keep only their latest numbers.
  • Connected accounts: when you disconnect an account, its stored statistics and auto-reply rules are deleted with it.
  • Posts, media, auto-reply logs and your account are kept while your account is active, and deleted within 30 days of your request to delete your account.
  • Waiting list details are kept until we have contacted you about your studio, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Your session expires after 24 hours, and signing out removes it from your phone.

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, every request is limited to the signed-in user's own data, and media is uploaded through short-lived signed links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you promptly if a breach affects you.

5. Your choices, and deleting your data

  • Disconnect accounts in the app under More, then Connected accounts. You can also remove our access from your Instagram, Facebook, Google or LinkedIn settings.
  • Delete posts, drafts and auto-reply rules in the app at any time.
  • Photos access can be turned off in your phone's settings; you just won't be able to attach media.
  • Delete your account and all its data: email us at info@changtechs.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and delete your account, connected accounts, statistics, posts, media and logs within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed when those backups expire.
  • See, correct or get a copy of your data, or withdraw your consent: email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR), you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

6. Children, changes and contact

Children. CMS Studio is a business tool for creators and brands and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes. If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app or by email before an important change takes effect.
